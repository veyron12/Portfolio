About

Mumbai, IN

I’m the only designer at Kodo, a YC W21 fintech. Engineers build a lot of the UI alongside me; my job is to set the direction, explain the why behind it, and own the final design decisions.

That covers everything from screen-by-screen UX audits and the design system to 0 → 1 products like Kodo North, our AI-native treasury and wealth platform. Dashboards, cards, payouts, approvals and investments have all gone through that process.

Before Kodo I spent four years at PayNearby, growing from design intern to senior product designer. I designed banking, insurance and payments flows used by retailers serving India’s underbanked customers, and helped take the design system to production.

I studied computer engineering, which helps me think about how things get built and talk through trade-offs with developers. I also use AI tools to move faster without cutting corners.
