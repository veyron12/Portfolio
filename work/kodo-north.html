Kodo North is Kodo’s AI-native treasury and wealth platform. It helps companies and individuals put surplus and idle cash to work, including in mutual funds, so the money is there exactly when it’s needed. Its assistant, North AI, creates reports, checks payables and receivables, and plans investments around them. I designed it from zero: name, brand, product, the AI experience and the website, shipped on a one-month timeline and presented at Mumbai Tech Week. It’s the broadest-scope project I’ve owned, and it’s still in active development.

Outcome

Brand, core flows, WhatsApp waitlist and website shipped in the first month, then presented at Mumbai Tech Week.

01

What North does

North helps companies and individuals plan how to invest their surplus and idle cash, including in mutual funds, so the money comes back at the right time. It brings together what’s coming in and what’s going out, so investment decisions are made with upcoming payments in view.
